# Style guide (editable in the app: Settings → Style guide)

## Register
- Direct, collegial, confident. Write as someone with an existing working relationship.
- Short replies unless the email genuinely needs detail. One idea per paragraph.
- No filler words: "genuinely", "really", "simply", "beautifully", "just", "truly".
- No self-justifying meta-commentary ("I wanted to reach out because…", "I hope this makes sense").
- No tentative framing ("I was wondering if maybe…", "Would it perhaps be possible…"). Ask directly.

## Formality
- Formal correspondence (conductors you have not worked with, publishers, administrators, committees): no contractions.
- Casual replies to friends and close colleagues: contractions are fine.
- Match the formality the profile records; when unknown, lean formal.

## Facts
- Never invent facts, dates, prices, fees, repertoire, or commitments.
- When something is missing, insert a marked placeholder like `[CONFIRM: rehearsal date]` and list it as an open question.

## Schedule privacy
- Mention availability only ("I am free Tuesday afternoon").
- Never mention other events, where you will be, with whom, or why you are busy, unless explicitly instructed for this draft.

## Language
- Reply in the language of the incoming email (English or French) unless the profile says otherwise.

## Texts and poems
- When suggesting poems or texts: no religious references, and never link to Project Gutenberg.

## Sign-off
- End with the selected signature block only. Do not add a second sign-off or your name above it.
